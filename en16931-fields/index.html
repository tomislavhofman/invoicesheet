EN 16931 business terms: UBL and CII XPath for every field

All 164 business terms of the European e-invoicing standard EN 16931 with the place they live in the two allowed syntaxes: UBL 2.1 (Peppol BIS, XRechnung UBL, eRačun, NLCIUS) and UN/CEFACT CII (XRechnung CII, ZUGFeRD, Factur-X).

Paths are absolute from the document root (/Invoice for UBL; for CII the root /rsm:CrossIndustryInvoice is left out). Line-level terms (BT-126 to BT-161) repeat once per invoice line. Conditions in square brackets tell apart terms that share an element, for example the seller VAT identifier (BT-31) and the seller tax registration (BT-32). For UBL credit notes, read CreditNote, CreditNoteLine and CreditedQuantity where the invoice uses Invoice, InvoiceLine and InvoicedQuantity.

Want to see these fields in your own files? Drop invoices into InvoiceSheet to get every invoice, line item and VAT rate as an Excel sheet, in your browser.
